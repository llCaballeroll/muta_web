Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag your finger to spin the paddle around your bacteria. Catch the good creatures and let the viruses go by. Filling the 3 slots with three of a kind, or three different ones, makes you mutate and gives you a power for 6 seconds. The full guide is in Settings → How to play.

I bought "Remove ads" and changed phones.

Open Settings → Restore purchases with the same Apple or Google account you used to buy it.

How do I get meteorites?

One free every day, up to five by watching ads, one for every 25 special creatures you catch, on the Road and with the stardust from duplicate cards.

What are the odds in a meteorite?

Each meteorite gives 5 cards. Each card is Common (60%), Rare (28%), Epic (10%) or Legendary (2%). The odds are shown in the Shop and on the home page.

Why is my bacteria sad?

If you don't play for a few days it gets hungry and then tired. Play a round to feed it.

I can't hear the music.

MUTA respects the iPhone's silent switch. Check that it isn't on silent and that music is on in Settings.

Can I move my progress to another phone?

For now, progress is saved only on your phone. Purchases can be recovered with "Restore purchases".

How do I see the ranking?

Tap Ranking on the home screen. On iPhone, sign in to Game Center in your phone Settings; on Android you're asked to sign in to Google Play Games when you tap the button.

How do I change my ad choices?

In Settings → Ad options. Read more in the Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

MUTA is a game created by Luis Angel Caballero Jardón ("we"). This policy explains what data is used when you play on iPhone, iPad or Android.

¿Prefieres español? Lee la política en español

In short

  • No accounts and no servers of our own. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • Ads are shown by Google AdMob. On iPhone we ask for tracking permission (ATT); in the regions that require it we show Google's consent form.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment details.

What we store

MUTA has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress (your bacteria's name, records, skins, meteorites and settings) is stored only on your phone. If you delete the app, that progress is deleted.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use device identifiers, your approximate IP address and ad usage data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

  • On iPhone we ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). If you don't give it, you'll still see ads, just less personalized.
  • In the European Union, the United Kingdom and other regions we show you Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice in Settings → Ad options.
  • More information: how Google uses data.

Purchases

Purchases (remove ads and meteorite packs) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We don't receive your payment details; only the confirmation that the purchase went through.

Ranking

If you use the ranking, your score and your alias are sent to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, under Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

Children

MUTA is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Since we don't keep your data on servers of our own, there's nothing we can look up or delete for you. For ad data, you can reset your phone's advertising identifier or withdraw your consent from the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above.

Contact

Write to us at soporte.mutagame@outlook.com.

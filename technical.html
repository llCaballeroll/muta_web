The foundation

Game engine

Flame runs Story, Time Attack and the playable tutorial: the orbiting paddle, the creatures, the mutation slots and the bosses.

Procedural bacteria

No sprites: every bacteria and creature is a blob path drawn each frame, with gradients, glow, patterns (spots, stripes, stars, sparkle) and a face. The skins on this site are generated from the same recipe.

Interface

Flutter (Cupertino) draws the home screen, My bacteria, the Shop, the Road and Settings, with Fredoka for the logo and numbers.

Meteorites

Each meteorite rolls 5 cards by rarity (60/28/10/2 %) among the 12 meteorite skins. Duplicates become stardust; 20 stardust become a meteorite.

Privacy by design

  • No sign-up: no name, email or phone number.
  • Local progress with shared_preferences; it's deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • No own server: the developer receives no match data.
  • Google AdMob with UMP consent and an "Ad options" button in Settings; if you decline consent, the app doesn't ask for tracking.
  • Apple and Google process purchases; Game Center and Google Play Games keep the leaderboards.

How to play

One finger.
Endless mutations.

Quick to learn, hard to put down.

  1. Spin Drag your finger to move the paddle around your bacteria.
  2. Catch Hit the creatures with the paddle before they reach the center. Every 3 in a row raises your combo.
  3. Mutate Fill the 3 slots. Three of a kind or three different ones trigger a power for 6 seconds.
  4. Dodge The spiky purple virus costs you a life in Story and time in Time Attack. Don't touch it.

Collection

20 skins.
Every one alive.

Every skin is drawn live by the game, so it wobbles, glows and sparkles. Seven are waiting on the Road and twelve come from meteorites.

Common Rare Epic Legendary
Each meteorite holds 5 cards. Each card drops with this chance and is split randomly among the meteorite skins of that rarity. Collect enough cards of a skin and it's yours. Cards of skins you already own turn into stardust, and 20 stardust become a new meteorite. The odds are also shown in the Shop.
